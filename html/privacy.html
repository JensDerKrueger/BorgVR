Summary
BorgVR has no ads, no user accounts, and no third-party tracking. The app stores datasets and settings locally unless you explicitly open remote data or start collaboration features.

What data the app processes

Datasets
BorgVR can open, import, cache, and store volume datasets on your device. Dataset files may contain scientific, medical, engineering, or other content chosen by you.
Visualization settings
The app stores settings such as render mode, transfer functions, isovalues, clipping state, background colors, server entries, and performance preferences locally.
Network access
If you open remote datasets or use collaboration, BorgVR uses network connections to load data, synchronize state, or make the currently open dataset available to participants.
SharePlay
SharePlay sessions can synchronize dataset identity, visualization settings, clipping state, and interaction state between participants. Dataset transfer may use local ad-hoc server connections.
Vision Pro permissions
On Apple Vision Pro, optional system permissions such as microphone, speech recognition, and world sensing may be used for voice interaction and spatial collaboration features.

What BorgVR does not do

No advertising
BorgVR does not show ads and does not use advertising SDKs.
No tracking
BorgVR does not use third-party tracking for marketing or cross-app profiling.
No account required
You do not need to create an account to use the app.
No automatic developer upload
BorgVR does not automatically upload your datasets to the developer. Network transfer happens only when you choose remote data or collaboration features.

Your control

Deletion
You can remove imported or cached datasets and settings from inside the app or by deleting the app, subject to normal system backup behavior.
Permissions
You can change or revoke system permissions at any time in iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or visionOS Settings.
Contact
If you have questions about privacy, please contact webmaster@jens-krueger.com.
